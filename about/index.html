How these are made

I record my sessions on the WoW: Forever beta. A pipeline I built reads the quest tracker, the quest windows, the objective messages and the minimap off the footage, finds each quest's accept, objectives and turn-in, and cuts a vertical video with captions, coordinates and the quest text read aloud. Quest facts are checked against Wowhead.
